Welcome to Sukhi Play

This turns the computer into a safe place for a small child to play. They get a few big buttons, and nothing else — no other websites, no popups, no way out without you.

Who is using the computer right now?

Choose what they can open

Pick one or two to start. You can add, remove or change these at any time from the grown-up screen.

Nothing here is installed or endorsed by Sukhi Play, and none of these sites is connected to it. You are responsible for checking each site's terms.

How you get back out

While your child is playing there is a small ✕ in the corner. Pressing it asks you to hold a button for three seconds — long enough that a toddler will not manage it.

You can also press Ctrl+Shift+X at any time. Try the hold now so you know what it feels like.

All set

One last thing: this reduces what a small child can reach, but it does not childproof a computer. Keep an eye on what they are playing.
