# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. This is a project about looking after small children, built mostly by
tired parents in the evenings. Act like it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient and welcoming, especially with people who are not developers. Many
  users here are parents who just want the thing to work.
- Assume good faith. Ask what someone meant before telling them they are wrong.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.
- Keep discussion on the technical or practical question at hand.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything targeting someone's
  religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexuality, or disability. This
  project has a Sikh child as its mascot; bigotry of any kind will get you
  removed without discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery. Children use this software.
- Posting a working kiosk escape publicly before it has been fixed. See
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that do not follow this, and may ban anyone for behaviour they consider
harmful.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact a maintainer privately. Reports
are handled confidentially and in good faith.
