Jazz's Studio

Design it. Print it. Make it yours.

Open the studio

For ages 7 and up · Free · No sign-up · Works without internet

What your child will see.

Where it came from

Made for one girl. Yours can play too.

Her little brother got Sukhi Play. Jazz, who is ten and loves stationery, wanted something of her own. So this is hers: her character, her colours, and a printer at the end of it.

Your child can use every part of it, with their own name on everything, and their own character too: the studio shows a grown-up how to make one from a photo, the way Jazz's was made.

What they can make

A whole stationery shop, printed at home

  • Stickers and labelsWith their name, their pattern, and a face on them
  • A diary that looks like theirsA cover with their name on it, and pages with a mood row
  • Planners and to-do listsA week at a glance, and boxes to tick
  • Make from a boxWraps that fit a toilet roll or a cereal box, with the steps
  • A brand for everyoneA logo for each person, business cards and logo stickers
  • Secret codes and moreNotes only a friend can read, name posters, story pages, invite cards and a doodle pad
Stickers, labels and tags

Everyone on them, each with their own name

Tick whoever goes on: Jazz, her brother Sukhi, a friend. Each one gets their own name under their face, changed right there on the sheet.

Make from a box

Turn the recycling into something

Measure a toilet roll, print a wrap that fits it, and it is a pencil pot. A drawing shows exactly what to measure, and two to a sheet can be for two people.

My brand

A logo for everyone, on everything

Each person has a brand: a badge, a stamp, a ribbon or a tag, with their picture. It goes on their diary, on business cards, and on logo stickers cut out round its shape.

Colours

Their colours, right where they are working

Twelve colour sets, dark ones first, and any colour besides. Try one and the sheet changes on the spot, and only that sheet: each one keeps its own.

The first time

It asks their name, and who they are

Two quick questions when it first opens: their name, so it says hello to them, and their character, Jazz, Sukhi, or one of their own. Both can be skipped and changed later.

Characters

Their family and friends, too

Add a character and it asks who it is. Each person has their own pictures, their own name on everything, and can be switched off without losing them.

Invite a friend

Tickets for their friends

Eight cards to cut out and hand round, each with a code a phone camera opens the studio from. The code is made on the device, with their character in the middle of it.

Story sparks

A story to write and draw

Who, what happens and where, chosen or spun: Sukhi finds a secret door on the moon. It prints as a page with a box to draw in, the hero already standing in it, and lines to write on.

My makes

Print it again, or change it first

After a print, the studio asks whether to keep it and what to call it. It keeps the settings, not a picture, so opening one brings the sheet back exactly as it was, ready to print or to change a word.

Printing

Comes out the right size

Everything is drawn on A4, to the millimetre, one sheet to a page.

  • Print at actual size Not "fit to page", so a wrap for a box goes round the thing it was measured for
  • Check with a ruler On a wrap, a line should measure 5 cm. If it does not, the printer shrank it
  • Inside Sukhi Play Pages go straight to your printer, with no print window a child could wander off through
Phones and tablets

Take it anywhere

The same studio on a small screen. Keep it on the home screen and it opens like an app, with no internet.

Add it to your home screen

  • iPhone and iPad Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android Open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.
  • Computer In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

  • No adsNo adverts, no pop-ups, nothing to buy
