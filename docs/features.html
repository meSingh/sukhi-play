Everything it does

One screen, locked to the sites you chose, that gives itself back when the time is up.

The screen is only what you chose

Sukhi Play fills the display. There is no address bar to type into, no new tab, no back button out of it, and no link that leads anywhere you did not allow. A child who taps everything finds only the tiles you put there.

0:30

Play time that ends by itself

Set how long a session lasts. It starts again every time the app opens, and stops on its own — no argument, because it was never your decision in the moment.

The countdown only appears in the last thirty seconds, so there is nothing to watch or bargain with.

1,284blocked

Adverts filtered

Advert and tracker requests are refused before they load, on every site you add. You can turn it off for a site whose terms ask you to — the child still cannot leave that site either way.

Hold

A gate a three-year-old cannot pass

Getting to the grown-up screen or quitting means holding a button for three seconds, or answering a small sum if your child has worked the hold out. Quick taps do nothing at all.

Apps that work with no internet at all

Three open-source apps ship inside the download — a colouring book, a keyboard playground and a set of first games. They are files on your disk, so they work on a train, in a car, and in a house with bad broadband. Nothing about them can reach the network, which also means there is nothing on them to filter.

Pictures your child saves land in your Pictures folder.

And the rest of it

Add any site by its address

Paste an address and Sukhi Play works out what the site needs, suggests a name and a picture, and lets you change all of it before you save.

A catalogue to start from

Sites known to work, already set up. Have a look — it is a compatibility list, not a recommendation.

No favicons, no logos

Every tile is a shape and a colour we drew. Nothing is fetched from the sites you add, so nothing on your screen is theirs to change.

More time without changing the rule

Grant fifteen minutes, an hour, or the rest of the day. It applies to this session only; tomorrow starts from your usual limit again.

Keyboard shortcuts held down

Alt+Tab, Alt+F4, Command+Q, Print Screen and the rest are caught while the window has focus. The one exception is the Windows key, and we say so plainly.

No downloads, no popups, no new windows

A site cannot open a window, start a download, or ask for your camera. The one thing that may write a file is a bundled app saving a picture.

Nothing is sent anywhere

No analytics, no accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting. The only request Sukhi Play makes on its own is checking GitHub for a newer version, and only when you press the button.

Free, and open source

MIT licensed. Every line is on GitHub, including the bits that decide what your child can reach.

Ready when you are

macOS, Windows and Linux. No account, no payment, no trial.
