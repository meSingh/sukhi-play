Everything it does

One screen, locked to the sites you chose, that gives itself back when the time is up. Every picture below is the real app.

The screen is only what you chose

Sukhi Play fills the display. There is no address bar to type into, no new tab, no back button out of it, and no link that leads anywhere you did not allow.

A child who taps everything finds only the tiles you put there.

A grown-up screen behind a real gate

Everything a parent decides is in one place, and a child cannot reach it. Getting in means holding a button for three seconds — or answering a small sum, if your child has worked the hold out.

Play time, the apps, the catalogue and every setting live here.

Play time that ends by itself

Set how long a session lasts. It starts again every time the app opens, and stops on its own — no argument, because it was never your decision in the moment.

When it ends you can grant fifteen minutes, an hour, or the rest of the day. Your usual limit does not move.

The countdown only appears in the last thirty seconds, so there is nothing to watch or bargain with.

A catalogue to start from

Sites that are known to work, already set up, with the hosts each one needs filled in. Three of them are not sites at all — a colouring book, a keyboard playground and a set of first games ship inside the download and work with no internet.

Have a look at the catalogue. It is a compatibility list, not a recommendation.

Adding your own takes a minute

Paste an address. Sukhi Play loads the site, works out which hosts it actually needs, and suggests a name and a picture. Change any of it before you save.

Advert filtering is on by default, and you can turn it off for a site whose terms ask you to.

It sets itself up the first time

The first run walks you through picking a few sites and choosing how you will get back in. After that it opens straight onto the tiles.

And the rest of it

Add any site by its address

Paste an address and Sukhi Play works out what the site needs, suggests a name and a picture, and lets you change all of it before you save.

No favicons, no logos

Every tile is a shape and a colour we drew. Nothing is fetched from the sites you add, so nothing on your screen is theirs to change.

More time without changing the rule

Grant fifteen minutes, an hour, or the rest of the day. It applies to this session only; tomorrow starts from your usual limit again.

Keyboard shortcuts held down

Alt+Tab, Alt+F4, Command+Q, Print Screen and the rest are caught while the window has focus. The one exception is the Windows key, and we say so plainly.

No downloads, no popups, no new windows

A site cannot open a window, start a download, or ask for your camera. The one thing that may write a file is a bundled app saving a picture.

Nothing is sent anywhere

No analytics, no accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting. The only request Sukhi Play makes on its own is checking for a newer version, and only when you press the button.

Free, and open source

MIT licensed. Every line is on GitHub, including the bits that decide what your child can reach.

Ready when you are

macOS, Windows and Linux. Free, no account, no payment, no trial.
