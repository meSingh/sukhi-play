# Jazz's Studio: stationery your child designs and prints at home

> Jazz's Studio lets children of seven and up design their own stickers, labels, bookmarks, diaries and more, with their own name and character, and print them at home.

By Mandeep Singh, 2026-09-24. Announcements. https://sukhiplay.com/blog/jazzs-studio/

The short version:

- Jazz's Studio is a stationery studio for children of about seven and up.
- They design stickers, labels, gift tags, bookmarks, door signs and diaries with their own name and character, and print them at home.
- It comes in Sukhi Play switched off, so a younger child's screen stays as it is until you add it.
- It also runs in any browser, an iPad included, and keeps everything on the device.

Sukhi Play started with my son, who is three and presses everything. His
sister Jazz is ten and loves stationery. Her little brother got Sukhi Play,
and she wanted something of her own. So I made her a studio, and it has her
name on the door.

Jazz's Studio is the first thing in Sukhi Play for older children, and yours
can use every part of it.

## What they can make

- **Stationery**: stickers, a diary, name labels, gift tags, planners and to-do
  lists, bookmarks and door signs. Each sheet keeps its own colours, lettering
  and pattern.
- **Make from a box**: measure a toilet roll or a cereal box, and print a wrap
  that turns it into a pencil pot, a desk tidy or a treasure box.
- **My brand**: a logo of their own, with business cards and logo stickers to
  match.
- **Play**: a poster of their name, secret messages in a real code with a key
  for a friend, story pages to write and draw, and invite cards with a code a
  friend's phone can scan.

![Me stickers of Jazz and Sukhi in turn, each with their own name underneath](/screenshots/studio/02-me-stickers.png "Everyone on one sheet, each with their own name.")

## It's theirs, not Jazz's

The first time it opens, the studio asks your child's name and who their
character is, so nobody else is greeted as Jazz. Her little brother Sukhi is in
there too, and families can add their own people: a picture of a character, a
name, and a brand of their own. A sheet of stickers or labels can have
everyone on it at once, each with their own words.

## Printed at home, on one page

Every sheet prints on one page, on any printer, an iPad included. After
printing, the studio offers to keep it in **My makes**, so next month's labels
are one tap away rather than made from scratch.

Inside Sukhi Play there's no print window at all, because a print window is a
way out of a locked screen. Only apps made for Sukhi Play may print, and they
go straight to your printer.

## Where to find it

- **In Sukhi Play** it comes in the download switched off, so a younger child's
  screen stays exactly as it is. Add **Studio** from the grown-up screen's
  catalogue ([how](/docs/jazz-studio/)). It needs no internet.
- **Anywhere else**, open it at [sukhiplay.com/playground/studio](/playground/studio/),
  on a computer, a tablet or a phone. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up
  for, and after one visit it works offline.

Everything your child makes stays on that device. There's no account, and
nothing is uploaded.

[The studio page](/studio/) shows every room. If your child is seven or older,
[open the studio](/playground/studio/) and see what they make first.

## Questions parents ask

### What age is Jazz's Studio for?

About seven and up. It comes in Sukhi Play switched off, so a younger child's screen stays exactly as it was until a parent adds it.

### Can my child use Jazz's Studio on an iPad?

Yes. Open sukhiplay.com/playground/studio/ in Safari. It prints one page, and Add to Home Screen keeps it there, working without internet after the first visit.

### Is anything my child makes uploaded?

No. Prints, photos and doodles are kept on that device only, and there is no account to make.
