# Jazz's Studio: stationery to design and print, for ages 7 and up

> Jazz's Studio, new in Sukhi Play 2.2 and grown up in 2.3, lets children of seven and up design and print their own stickers, labels, diaries and more.

By Mandeep Singh, 2026-09-24. Announcements. https://sukhiplay.com/blog/jazzs-studio/

Jazz's Studio is a stationery studio for children of about seven and up: they design stickers, labels, bookmarks, gift tags, door signs and diaries with their own name and characters on them, and print them at home. It arrived in Sukhi Play 2.2.0 and grew up in 2.3.0. It is free, works without internet, and also runs in any browser at [sukhiplay.com/playground/studio](/playground/studio/).

## Why I made it

Sukhi Play started for my son, Sukhi, who is under four and presses every key and clicks everything. His sister Jazz is ten and loves stationery, so I made her a studio, and it has her name on it.

It is the first thing in Sukhi Play for older children, and yours can use all of it.

## What can children make in Jazz's Studio?

- **Stationery**, in five groups: stickers, a diary cover and pages, name labels and gift tags, a week planner and to-do lists, and bookmarks and door signs. Each sheet keeps its own colours, lettering and pattern.
- **Make from a box**: pick a pencil pot, desk tidy, notebook cover or treasure box, measure the toilet roll or cereal box, and print a wrap that fits it, with the steps.
- **My brand**: a logo for each person, as a badge, stamp, ribbon or tag, with business cards and logo stickers cut out round the logo's shape.
- **Play**: a poster of their name, secret messages in a real code with a key for a friend, story pages, a doodle pad, and invite cards.
- **My makes**: their prints, kept to open again, and a shelf of the things they made, each with a photo and a note.
- **Make it yours**: their character, their name, and the studio's colours, lettering and background.

## What is new in 2.3?

2.2.0 brought the studio into Sukhi Play. 2.3.0, out the same day, made it properly anyone's.

- **It asks who they are.** The first time it opens, it asks the child's name and their character, so nobody else is greeted as Jazz. Both can be skipped and changed later.
- **Sukhi joins Jazz**, with seven characters of his own.
- **Families add their own characters.** A picture of a character on one plain colour goes in, the background is cut out on the device, and the studio asks who it is. Each person has their own pictures, their own name and their own brand, and can be switched off without losing them.
- **Everyone on one sheet.** Stickers, labels, bookmarks, gift tags, door signs and box wraps take several people at once, each with their own words.
- **Invite cards.** Eight to a sheet, each with a QR code a friend's phone camera opens the studio from. The code is made on the device, with their character in the middle of it.
- **Story pages.** Who, what happens and where, chosen or spun, printed with a box to draw in and lines to write on.

## Keeping a print to use again

After a print, the studio asks whether to keep it in **My makes** and what to call it. It keeps the settings rather than a picture, so **Open** brings the sheet back exactly as it was, ready to print again or to change a word first.

## How does printing work?

Every sheet is drawn on A4 to the millimetre and prints on one page, on any printer, an iPad included. Print box wraps at actual size, not "fit to page"; each has a line that should measure exactly 5 cm. An iPad always prints a little smaller, which is fine for stickers but worth measuring for a wrap.

**Inside Sukhi Play there is no print window.** A print window can save a file or open another program, and both are ways out of the locked screen. So Sukhi Play sends the page straight to the computer's default printer, and the studio tells your child when it has gone, or to ask a grown-up if the printer did not answer. Only apps Sukhi Play made itself can print; a website that tries is refused.

## Where do I find it?

- **In Sukhi Play** it comes in the download but is switched off, so a younger child's screen stays as it was. Open the grown-up screen, go to its catalogue, and add **Studio** ([how](/docs/jazz-studio/)). It works with no internet at all.
- **On any other device**, open [/playground/studio/](/playground/studio/) in a browser. Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. After one visit it works offline, and it can be added to the home screen of a phone or tablet.

Everything is kept on that device only. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Clearing the browser's data for the site clears it too.

One honest note: the studio has a guide for grown-ups on making a character from a photo using Google Gemini. If you use Gemini, the photo goes to Google. Nothing you add to the studio itself leaves the device.

The [studio page](/studio/) has pictures of every part, and the [Jazz's Studio docs](/docs/jazz-studio/) go through printing, characters and where things are kept. If you want the studio inside Sukhi Play, it is on the [download page](/download/).

## Questions parents ask

### What age is Jazz's Studio for?

About seven and up. It comes in Sukhi Play switched off, so a younger child's screen stays exactly as it was until a parent adds it.

### Can my child use Jazz's Studio on an iPad?

Yes. Open sukhiplay.com/playground/studio/ in Safari. It prints one A4 page, and Add to Home Screen keeps it there, working without internet after the first visit.

### Is anything my child makes uploaded?

No. Prints, photos and doodles are kept on that device only, and there is no account to make.
