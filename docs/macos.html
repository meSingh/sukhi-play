The first launch is blocked

“Sukhi Play.app” Not Opened. Apple could not verify it is free of malware.

Expected. Apple charges to vouch for an app, and this one is free, so macOS says the strongest thing it can.

  1. Click Done. You have to try once for the next step to appear.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to Security at the bottom.
  4. Click Open Anyway and enter your password.

Once per install. Comfortable in Terminal? This skips the dialog:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Sukhi Play.app"

Older Macs

macOS 10.15, 11 and 12 can’t run the normal build. Electron, which the app is built on, needs macOS 13.

ElectronRuns on
30, 31, 32macOS 10.15 and later
33 to 37macOS 11 and later
44, the normal buildmacOS 13 and later

The older build is not security patched. It uses Electron 32, which has known, unfixed vulnerabilities.

Apple stopped patching macOS 10.15 in 2022, so such a Mac is already there, and Sukhi Play only opens sites you approved. On macOS 13 or later, take the normal download.
