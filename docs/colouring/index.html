Phones and tablets

Just as good on a small screen

Same pictures, same pens, with buttons sized for small fingers.

Add it to your home screen

  • iPhone and iPad Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android Open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.
  • Computer In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

Or press Add to home screen inside the app for the steps on your device. It then opens like any other app, full screen, even with no internet.
