Setting up Sukhi Play used to mean knowing which sites to add, and it needed an
internet connection for everything. 2.0 takes care of both. You can pick sites
from a ready-made list, and three apps now come inside the download, so there
is always something to play, signal or no signal.

You'll find it as 2.0.2 on the download page. A hiccup while publishing used up
the first numbers, so that is the one to install.

## Sites in one tap

The grown-up screen now has a **Catalogue** tab: sites known to work well in
Sukhi Play, each on its own card. A card tells you up front whether the site
has adverts, and whether Sukhi Play filters them. Tap **Add** and it's on your
child's tiles, already set up. Nine sites are in it to start with.

The same list is on this website, as [the catalogue](/catalogue/), so you can
look before you download. And any site that isn't there can still be added by
typing its address.

## Apps that work with no internet

This is the part I'm happiest about. Three apps now come inside the download
and run from your own computer, so they work on a train, on a plane, or when
the broadband is down:

- **Colouring**: colour in animals, flowers and rockets, or just draw. A saved
  picture lands in a Sukhi Play folder in your Pictures.
- **Keyboard** ([Magic Smash](https://github.com/Evandro/MagicSmash)): press
  any key and something happens on screen, so keyboard bashing is the whole game.
- **First Games** (Little Explorer): counting, colours, shapes and animals,
  with nothing to read.

They're switched on from the start, so a fresh install already has something
to play. Each one can only read its own files, and nothing else on your
computer.

## A simpler grown-up screen

Everything a grown-up needs now sits on four tabs: **Play time**, **Installed
apps**, **Catalogue** and **Settings**.

- **Play time** can be any length up to four hours, and when it runs out your
  child can ask for more. You decide: fifteen minutes, an hour, or the rest of
  the day, just for that session.
- **Getting in** can be the three-second hold, or a small sum instead, for the
  day your child works out the hold.
- **Tiles** are one of sixteen shapes in a colour you choose, and while a site
  loads your child sees their tile's colour instead of a blank screen.
