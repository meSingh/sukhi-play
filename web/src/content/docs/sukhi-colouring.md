Sukhi Colouring is the colouring book that comes with Sukhi Play. It is the
first app in the [playground](/playground/): little apps we make ourselves for
Sukhi Play, rather than websites you allow. More are on the way.

## Where to find it

- **In Sukhi Play** it is the **Colouring** tile, switched on from the start.
  It is part of the download, so it works with no internet at all.
- **On any other device** open
  [sukhiplay.com/playground/colouring](/playground/colouring/) in a browser.
  Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

## What your child can do

- **Pick a picture** with the pictures button: forty to choose from, or leave
  it and draw on a blank page.
- **Draw** with the pencil, the crayon or the rainbow, in three widths.
- **Tap to fill** a shape inside the picture. Tapping the paper around it
  colours the whole page instead.
- **Mirror drawing**, the butterfly, copies everything onto the other side.
- **Rub out** with the eraser under the colours.
- **Back** and **Forward** undo and redo. **Start again** clears the page, and
  asks first.

## Saving their pictures

Press **Keep it** and the picture goes into **My pictures**. Open one from there
and it comes back exactly as it was left, ready to carry on colouring. The
download button under each saves a copy as an image file, and the bin removes
it, after asking.

Pictures are kept **on that device only**. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no
account for them to be tied to. My pictures holds the sixty most recent; the
oldest make way for new ones. Clearing the browser's data for the site, or
using a private window, clears them too.

## Settings

The cog holds the language, English or 한국어, and the sound on or off. It sits
at the end of the tools, out of the way of the ones a child uses most.

## On a phone or tablet

It works the same on a small screen, with the tools along the top and the
colours along the bottom.

To keep it on the home screen, press **Add to home screen** in the app: the
phone with an arrow in it, next to the cog. It shows the steps for the device
you are on, and in Chrome or Edge it has an **Install** button that does it in
one tap. The steps, in case you would rather do it yourself:

- **iPhone and iPad:** open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then
  **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, open the menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
- **A computer:** in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the end of the
  address bar.

It then opens full screen like any other app, and keeps working without
internet once it has been opened once.
